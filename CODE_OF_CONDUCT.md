# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for educators, learners, researchers, and developers.

Be constructive, protect learner privacy, distinguish evidence from inference, and assume good faith while remaining precise about technical risks. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, publication of private learner material, and hostile conduct are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community or learner data.
